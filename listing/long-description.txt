Inkline lets you write notes in scripts without fonts, whether you're deciphering an invented language in a game, creating a conlang, or using a cipher or shorthand.

Draw symbols with a mouse or trackpad, or trace or convert screenshots into ink. Symbols appear inline at the text's size and color. Name them, record readings and translations, and edit these later without changing your notes. Type ;; (configurable) to find and insert symbols by name, reading, or translation. Search your vault for notes using them.

Symbols are ordinary PNG files embedded with standard image links, so notes remain readable without Inkline. No account or network access is needed. Inkline only reads vault files and images you explicitly provide. Unsaved drawings are stored locally as Obsidian drafts.

Desktop only.

Inkline is written and maintained by AI agents. No human reviews the code.
